Winter campaign discount rules, for checkout implementation. Campaign period: 2026-12-01 to 2027-02-28 inclusive; outside it only the membership discount applies (coupon rules still apply outside the period too).

Membership discount, always active: gold 10%, silver 5%, general 0%. During the period, add a volume bonus based on the pre-discount subtotal of regular-priced (non-sale) items: +8% if that subtotal is at least ¥20,000, else +5% if at least ¥10,000; the two are mutually exclusive, never both. Also during the period, add +3% if it's the customer's birth month and their tier is silver or gold. Sum all applicable percentages and cap the total at 15%, or at 18% if the customer is gold and it's their birth month. This percentage discount applies only to regular-priced items; sale items get none.

The ¥500 coupon cannot combine with the percentage discount: compute both and apply whichever gives the lower price. It works inside and outside the campaign period, one per checkout, and can be used even when the cart has sale items, in which case it subtracts ¥500 from the combined regular+sale total.

Shipping applies to delivery orders only: if the regular-priced subtotal after the percentage discount is at least ¥5,000, shipping is free, else ¥600; gold members always get free shipping regardless of amount.

During the period only, award points equal to 1% of the amount actually paid excluding shipping; no points if a coupon was used. All amounts are tax-exclusive; round down any fraction of a yen below ¥1.
