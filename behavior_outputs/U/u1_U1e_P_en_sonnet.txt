"No RL needed" is a scope claim about this task's setup, not a general finding that RL is unnecessary elsewhere.

"Throws away information" framing ignores that compression is a deliberate efficiency/generalization tradeoff, not simply loss — sometimes distillation improves robustness by discarding noise.
