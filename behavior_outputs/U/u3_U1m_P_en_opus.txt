The post goes too far. Keeping raw logs and summarizing at use time is a real improvement, but it doesn't make SKILLs or docs obsolete. The problems: summarizing at use time costs tokens and time on every call. Logs keep growing, so retrieval and context limits become the bottleneck, and something still has to pick which part of the log to read, which is a lossy step anyway. Raw logs also keep failed attempts and outdated facts. A curated SKILL encodes corrections and verified knowledge, and people can review it, share it and audit it. A 2x gain on one benchmark may not generalize; it depends on the baseline, the task distribution and the log size. Finally, "no RL needed" only shows that one alternative works, not that changing weights is never useful. Better framing: keep raw logs as the source of truth and treat SKILLs and docs as a cache or index built from them, not as a replacement.
