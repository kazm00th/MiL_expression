SQLite suffices IF writes are low-concurrency (30 users clocking in/out, short transactions, WAL mode), app and DB stay on one server, no need for DB-level roles or network access, and simple ops are valued (backup = copy file, e.g. litestream). Choose PostgreSQL IF you expect write bursts (everyone clocks in at 9:00), multiple app processes or servers later, heavy reporting or concurrent long queries, strict types and constraints, row-level permissions, or integration with BI or other tools over the network. At this scale performance is not the deciding factor; growth plans and ops skill are. Default: SQLite plus WAL now, use an ORM so you can migrate later. Do you expect to scale beyond one server or connect other systems?
