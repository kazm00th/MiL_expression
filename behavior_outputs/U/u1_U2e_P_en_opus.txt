SQLite is enough for your case. At 30 users, attendance writes are small and infrequent, so SQLite's one-writer-at-a-time limit won't matter. Turn on WAL mode, set busy_timeout to a few seconds and run scheduled backups with the .backup command or Litestream. Switch to PostgreSQL IF any of these apply: you'll add a second app server or a separate worker process; other tools such as BI or reporting need to reach the database over the network; you need database-level user permissions or auditing; you expect growth well past internal scale; or your team already runs Postgres and has the ops habits for it. Otherwise, SQLite means one fewer service to run and back up. If you think you might switch later, use an ORM and avoid SQLite-specific SQL.
